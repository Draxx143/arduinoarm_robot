Getting ConnectedQUICK START

  1. Flash RobotArm_Firmware.ino onto the Mega 2560.
  2. Connect the board over USB. Close the Arduino Serial Monitor first!
  3. Click “⚡ Connect Arduino” and pick the COM port.
  4. Press “⌂ Home All” to calibrate the axes.
  5. No hardware? Use the built-in ▦ Simulator to explore everything.

⚠ Safety: clear the arm’s path before homing and keep the E-STOP within reach at all times.
